01 — Foundation

What is an AI Agent?

An AI Agent is a software entity that perceives its environment, makes autonomous decisions, uses tools, and takes actions to achieve a specific goal — all without requiring constant human instruction.

04 — Evolution

Agentic vs Traditional AI

⛔ TRADITIONAL AI
❌ Single-turn: one question, one answer
❌ No memory between interactions
❌ Cannot use tools or APIs
❌ Needs human to break tasks down
❌ Passive — only responds to prompts
❌ No awareness of errors or results
VS
✅ AGENTIC AI
✅ Multi-step: plans and executes sequences
✅ Persistent memory across sessions
✅ Calls external tools & real-world APIs
✅ Autonomously decomposes complex goals
✅ Proactive — initiates actions
✅ Self-corrects based on feedback
